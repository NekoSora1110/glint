Privacy Policy

Glint by Starex · Last updated October 25, 2026

Short version: Glint keeps the least it can: an anonymous sign-in ID, your subscription status and how many questions you've asked. We don't keep the text of your questions or answers, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell anything about you.

What we keep

WhatWhyHow long
Your Sign in with Apple ID. This is a random ID from Apple. Glint does not ask for your name or email.To know it's you and keep your planUntil you delete your account

Your watchlist and app settings are saved only on your iPhone.

Who helps us run Glint

To answer a question, Glint sends only what's needed to these service providers:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): your question, your last few questions in the same conversation, and the stock data needed to answer. Not your Apple ID or any account details. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data isn't used to train its models.
  • Finnhub and Twelve Data (market data): stock symbols only, like "AAPL".
  • Supabase: runs Glint's servers and database in the United States.
  • Apple: handles Sign in with Apple and all payments. We never see your card or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of any data.
  • No storing of the text of your questions or Glint's answers on our servers.

Deleting your data

In Glint, go to Account › Delete account. This deletes your account, subscription record and question history from our servers. You can also remove Glint from your Apple ID in Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple. Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription. Cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Children

Glint is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Not financial advice

Glint is an educational tool. It explains stocks but does not give investment advice or recommendations, and it does not trade or hold money.

Changes

If we change what Glint does with data, we'll update this page and the date above before the change goes live.
