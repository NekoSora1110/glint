Common questions

Is Glint financial advice?
No. Glint explains stocks to help you learn: what a company does, why a price moved, what the risks are. It never tells you to buy or sell, and nobody can promise what a stock will do. For decisions about your own money, talk to a licensed financial professional.

Can Glint predict stocks?
Glint's AI gives an outlook: which way it leans, how sure it is (low or medium, never more), rough good and bad scenarios, and why. It's a general guess, the same for everyone, and it is often wrong. Nobody can reliably predict stock prices, including AI.

Where do the numbers come from?
Live prices, charts and news are shown by TradingView and can be delayed. Company figures (sales, profit, cash, debt) come from each company's official SEC filings. Explanations are written by an AI (Claude, by Anthropic), which can also search the web for recent news and prices and says the date of each one. AI can make mistakes, so check anything important.

How many questions can I ask?
Glint Plus: 60 a month. Glint Pro: 180 a month (both reset on the 1st, UTC). Glint Max: 60 every week, billed weekly (resets Monday, UTC). Questions that aren't about stocks don't count.

How do I cancel or change my plan?
In Glint: Account › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Glint. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

I got a new phone and my plan is gone.
Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Restore purchases on the plan screen (or Account › Restore purchases).

How do I delete my account?
Account › Delete account. This removes your Glint account and question history. It does not cancel your Apple subscription, so cancel that too.
