Common questions

Is Glint financial advice?
No. Glint explains stocks to help you learn: what a company does, why a price moved, what the risks are. It never tells you to buy or sell, and nobody can promise what a stock will do. For decisions about your own money, talk to a licensed financial professional.

Where do the numbers come from?
Prices, charts and company facts come from market data providers (Finnhub and Twelve Data) and can be delayed. Explanations are written by an AI (Claude, by Anthropic) using that data and, for news questions, a quick web search. AI can make mistakes, so check anything important.

How many questions can I ask?
Glint Plus: 25 a week and 80 a month. Glint Pro: 75 a week and 250 a month. Weeks reset on Monday and months on the 1st (UTC). Questions that aren't about stocks don't count.

How do I cancel or change my plan?
In Glint: Account › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Glint. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

I got a new phone and my plan is gone.
Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Restore purchases on the plan screen (or Account › Restore purchases).

How do I delete my account?
Account › Delete account. This removes your Glint account and question history. It does not cancel your Apple subscription, so cancel that too.
